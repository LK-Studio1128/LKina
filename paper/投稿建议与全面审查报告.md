### 1.4 Cover Letter 模板

```
Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript, "LKina: A Metal-Aware and
Covalent-Reactive Molecular Docking Engine Extending AutoDock Vina,"
for consideration as a Full Article in the Journal of Chemical
Information and Modeling.

Metalloenzymes mediate ~40% of enzyme reactions in the human proteome
and are targeted by ~one-third of approved drugs, yet mainstream
docking force fields cannot describe metal–ligand coordination
directionality. LKina is an open-source engine derived from AutoDock
Vina 1.2.7 that addresses this gap at the engine level: it extends
the AD4 atom-type system to 113 types (covering 80+ metals), embeds
an independent AutoGrid 4.2 grid generator (removing the external
autogrid4 dependency), and adds a four-tier reactive covalent docking
framework (P1–P4) with NAC detection, a C3 two-phase strategy, and
six reaction presets.

All claims are backed by reproducible measurements: a 110-mode
synthetic metal-coordination benchmark (LKina docks 110/110 vs.
34/110 for Vina 1.2.7, mean |d–d0| = 0.20 Å), a PDB-derived
redocking benchmark across Zn²⁺/Fe³⁺/Cu²⁺ metalloproteins (104
systems; with the coordination model active, 90.6% of best poses
place a ligand donor within 3.0 Å of the metal vs. 27.8% without and
52.1% for Vina), and end-to-end tests of every feature family
(pseudoatoms, BVS, water bridge, metal-as-ligand) and all six
covalent presets. The engine reproduces Vina 1.2.7 energies exactly
on metal-free systems, and the full benchmark pipeline ships in the
repository (GPL-3.0-or-later, tagged v1.0.1).

We believe the manuscript fits JCIM's scope as the natural venue for
AutoDock-ecosystem methods (AutoDock4Zn, MetalDock, and Vina 1.2.0
were published here) and will be of broad interest to the molecular
docking and metalloenzyme communities.

We suggest the following potential reviewers: [3–5 names with

ecosystem]. The manuscript has not been published or submitted
elsewhere. All authors approve the submission and declare no
competing interests.
```

### 2.5 总体评价

**优点（可保持）：**
1. 证据链完整闭环——每个数字可复现，这是审稿人最看重的"软件论文"品质。
